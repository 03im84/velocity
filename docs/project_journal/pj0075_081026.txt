Problema:

Input publica VehicleControlCommand, mientras Propulsion, Steering y Brake
consumen Commands especializados. Falta el Managed Runtime controller que
valide, traduzca y publique esos tres contratos sin aplicar física.

Decisión:

Añadir VehicleControlRouterRuntimeUnit con PropulsionIntentPolicy BORROWED
y DeviceBus recibido durante runtime_initialize.

Contrato:

profile_id: velocity.vehicle_control.router
profile_version: 1
activation_context: SIMULATION
capability: vehicle_control_routing
input: in.vehicle_control_command
publishes: propulsion_command, steering_command, brake_command
subscribes: vehicle_control_command

Routing:

- validate BusMessage/Topic/timestamp/payload;
- validate VehicleControlCommand and timestamp agreement;
- calculate normalized thrust through policy;
- create PropulsionCommand, SteeringCommand and BrakeCommand;
- prebuild three BusMessages with Router source and shared timestamp;
- publish Propulsion, then Steering, then Brake;
- preserve last successful Commands and ValidationReport.

Publication failure:

DeviceBus publications are sequential dispatch cycles. On rejection, Router
stops later publications and reports PLATFORM_SAFETY_ERROR. It does not
pretend atomic rollback for messages already dispatched. Partial publication
never populates last-success Commands.

No responsabilidades:

- force or torque calculation;
- motion sources;
- sinks;
- RigidBody;
- physical coordination;
- policy ownership.

Cobertura diseñada:

- exact Unit/Configuration/policy validation;
- lifecycle and exact endpoint;
- three specialized publications;
- exact order/source/timestamps/payload types;
- throttle/brake policy mapping;
- independent steering/brake mapping;
- full-brake reverse suppression;
- message/topic/timestamp/payload rejection;
- no output for invalid input;
- ordered publication stop on failure;
- partial publication not reported as success;
- idempotent shutdown;
- no post-shutdown publication;
- BORROWED policy preservation.
