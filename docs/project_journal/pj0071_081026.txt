Problema:

VehicleControlCommand contiene brake intent, pero no existe un snapshot
especializado para enrutar active braking hacia un futuro BrakeRuntimeUnit
sin reutilizar PropulsionCommand ni representar reverse thrust.

Decisión:

Añadir `BrakeCommand` read-only y su Topic canónico en la misma entrega.

Contrato:

normalized_brake: finite float in [0, 1]
timestamp: finite float >= 0
Topic: BusTopics.BRAKE_COMMAND = &"brake_command"

Responsabilidades:

- preservar normalized brake;
- preservar timestamp;
- validar finitud y bounds;
- exponer getters read-only.

No responsabilidades:

- DeviceBus publication;
- routing;
- brake force calculation;
- deadzone;
- velocity reading;
- physics;
- sink ownership;
- RigidBody;
- mutation API.

Implementación:

core/bus/bus_topics.gd

test/core/braking/brake_command_test.gd
test/core/braking/BrakeCommandTest.tscn

Cobertura diseñada:

- valid command;
- zero/full/partial boundaries;
- positive overflow and negative input;
- NAN and infinities;
- negative/non-finite timestamp;
- canonical Topic type/value;
- read-only API;
- no Bus or physics responsibility.
