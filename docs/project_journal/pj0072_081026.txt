Problema:

VehicleControlCommand contiene throttle y brake. El futuro Router debe
producir PropulsionCommand sin ocultar la interacción longitudinal dentro
del RuntimeUnit, Propulsion model o root scene.

Decisión:

Añadir una policy pura/stateless que combine throttle y brake sin conocer
Commands, Bus o física.

Contrato:

calculate_normalized_thrust(throttle, brake) -> float

Formula:

bounded_throttle = clamp(throttle, -1, 1)
bounded_brake = clamp(brake, 0, 1)
normalized_thrust = bounded_throttle * (1 - bounded_brake)

Safety:

- finite out-of-range inputs clamp;
- non-finite throttle fails neutral to 0.0;
- non-finite brake fails neutral to 0.0;
- output is revalidated and clamped to [-1, 1];
- full brake suppresses forward and reverse throttle.

No responsabilidades:

- VehicleControlCommand validation;
- specialized Command construction;
- DeviceBus publication;
- routing lifecycle;
- force calculation;
- sink or RigidBody ownership;
- mutable state.
