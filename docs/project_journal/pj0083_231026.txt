Problema:

SteeringRuntimeUnit produce signed scalar yaw torque, pero falta el adapter
Godot que aplique ese torque alrededor del craft body-up axis sin dar el
RigidBody3D al Runtime.

Decisión:

Añadir `RigidBodySteeringTorqueSink`, scene-owned RefCounted con un body
explícito BORROWED, que aplica torque signed sobre body local +Y expresado en
world coordinates.

Contrato:

apply_yaw_torque(torque_newton_meters, timestamp) -> bool

- body válido y dentro de SceneTree;
- body no frozen (activation gate hasta ACTIVE);
- basis finita, positiva/no reflejada y no degenerada;
- torque finito y signed;
- timestamp finito y >=0;
- body_up = normalized orthonormalized(global_basis).y;
- world_torque = body_up * signed torque;
- body.apply_torque(world_torque);
- zero torque aceptado como actuation neutral explícita;
- no state, queues, timers, Bus o source discovery;
- no body getter/setter;
- no aplica fuerzas lineales.

Alternativas:

1. Aplicar torque vector desde SteeringRuntimeUnit.
   Rechazada: filtra coordenadas y RigidBody a Runtime.
2. Aplicar torque desde VehicleCompositionRoot.
   Rechazada: Root acumularía actuator responsibility.
3. Sink especializado scene-owned alrededor de body-up.
   Seleccionada: preserva scalar boundary y separa torque de fuerzas.

Production assertions passed:

- positive torque accelerates about rotated body-up;
- negative torque produces negative body-up yaw rate.

Two fixture assertions failed after physics integration:

- no right-axis angular velocity;
- no linear velocity from pure torque.

Root cause:

The test helper created multiple active BoxShape RigidBody3D fixtures at the
same world origin and left earlier bodies in the physics world. They collided
with the current body during awaited physics frames, contaminating measured
angular and linear velocity. This is test-fixture interference, not a sink
behavior defect.

r1 correction:

- test-only change spaces fixture bodies 5 meters apart;
- production sink remains byte-identical;
- both actuation-direction assertions remain unchanged.
